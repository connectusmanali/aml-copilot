# Structuring

## Definition
Structuring means splitting cash into several transactions below a reporting threshold to avoid a report. In Canada, cash of CAD 10,000 or more received in one transaction, or in several transactions within 24 hours by or for the same person, must be reported as a large cash transaction. Deliberately staying under that line is itself a red flag.

## Red flags
Several cash payments between 9,000 and 9,999 within a day or two. Amounts that vary slightly to look random. The same counterparty receiving each payment. Cash activity that does not fit the customer's declared occupation or expected volume.

## Investigation steps
List every cash payment by the subject in the 48 hours around the alert. Check whether amounts cluster just under the threshold. Compare total cash with expected monthly volume from KYC. Ask whether a legitimate business reason (for example a cash-intensive retail business) explains the pattern.

## Common false positives
A single honest cash deposit just under the threshold, such as a used car sale, is not structuring on its own. Look for repetition.
