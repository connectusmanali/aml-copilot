# Fan-out and money mules

## Definition
Fan-out is one account sending funds to many accounts in a short time. Launderers use it to spread proceeds across money mules: accounts held by recruited or complicit people who forward the funds.

## Red flags
Payments to five or more new counterparties within hours. Similar amounts to each recipient. Recipients who quickly move the money on. A sender whose normal activity is low.

## Investigation steps
List the subject's outgoing payments in the 24 hours around the alert and count distinct recipients. Check whether recipients are new relationships. Check recipients against the watchlist and for onward movement.

## Common false positives
Payroll: a business paying many employees the same day, usually by ACH, in regular amounts, on a regular schedule. Check the customer segment and whether the pattern repeats monthly.
