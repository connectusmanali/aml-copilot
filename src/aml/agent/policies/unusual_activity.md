# Unusual activity and KYC mismatch

## Definition
Activity that departs sharply from the customer's own history or from what they declared at onboarding: a sudden spike in outflows, or monthly volume several times the declared expectation.

## Red flags
24-hour outflow many times the account's 30-day daily average. 30-day outflow at least three times the declared monthly volume. A new account with large, fast activity.

## Investigation steps
Compare current activity with the account's own baseline and with the KYC declaration. Check the account's age. Record any explanation that should be confirmed with the customer as an open question.

## Common false positives
Life events such as a house sale, inheritance or a new job can legitimately change volumes. Under-declaration at onboarding is common and is a reason to ask, not proof of laundering.
