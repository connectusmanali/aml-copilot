# High-risk customers

## Definition
Customers rated high risk at onboarding because of their business type, geography or other factors. Money service businesses, import and export firms, car dealerships and real estate holding companies are commonly treated as higher risk because they handle large values or cash.

## Red flags
Large payments by a high-risk customer that do not fit the stated business. Sanctions, politically exposed person or adverse media matches on the customer or a counterparty.

## Investigation steps
Confirm the risk rating and its reasons. Screen the subject and key counterparties against the watchlist. A confirmed sanctions match must be escalated immediately and never closed by an analyst.
